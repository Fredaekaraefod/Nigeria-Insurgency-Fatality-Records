Boko Haram Conflict Registry

2009 — 2026

What is this? This is an open database of 831 verified Boko Haram conflict incidents recorded across Nigeria and the Lake Chad Basin from 2009 to 2026. Records are sourced from the Council on Foreign Relations Nigeria Security Tracker, Wikipedia monthly timelines, and Amnesty International reports.

How to use it: Type any keyword below — a state name, attack type, year, or location — to instantly filter the incident table. Two charts above the table will automatically update to reflect your search. You can also click the quick-filter buttons or the search suggestions below.
